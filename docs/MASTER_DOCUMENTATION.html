🌟 1. Product Manifesto & Core Philosophy

WatchersHive (brand: WatchHive) is a warm, social movie and TV show tracking network designed for people who truly care about cinema and shared entertainment.

Unlike corporate streaming platforms that isolate viewers or text-heavy legacy databases (Letterboxd, IMDb) that focus on solitary reviewing, WatchersHive combines four foundational pillars:

⚡

Frictionless Action

Logging finished watches or tracking active episodes takes under 3 seconds with instant optimistic UI response.

🤝

Anti-Algorithm Social

Real recommendations from people whose taste you actually trust, attributed permanently with a closed gratitude feedback loop.

🏆

Cinephile Stacks

Dynamic, reorderable ranked collections that act as your living cinematic report card rather than forgotten static playlists.

🧠

MindLens Intelligence

Mathematical reflections of your cognitive viewing patterns (Shannon Information Theory and Affective Valence) without horoscopes.

🎬 4. Complete Feature Catalog & User Flows

4.1 Watch Tracking & Rewatch Engine

The watch logger is the central heart of WatchersHive. It captures completed viewings with fine-grained context:

  • Dual Watch Modes: Finished (WATCHED) vs Currently Watching (WATCHING).
  • 10-Point Rating Parity: 0.5 to 10.0 scale, mapped to 5 honey stars with half-star increments.
  • Viewing Context Tags: Cinema, Home, Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+ Hotstar, Apple TV+, etc.
  • Spoiler Masking: User reviews flagged with spoilers are blurred by default with explicit click-to-reveal.
  • Duplicate Watch Detection: Automatically identifies repeat viewings, prompting between logging a fresh Rewatch or modifying the earlier log.

4.2 Currently Watching Shelf

Surfaced prominently at the top of user profiles and library screens. Features an atomic Move-to-Watched action that pre-fills viewing start time, sets completion timestamp, and opens rating without re-entering details.

4.3 Granular TV Season & Episode Tracking

A complete series tracking subsystem supporting multi-season shows:

  • Episode Checklist: Expandable seasons listing every episode with thumbnail, overview, and instant check-off toggle.
  • One-Tap Batch Catch-Up: Catching up on season 3? Tap one button to mark all prior episodes (S01E01 to S03E05) as watched simultaneously.
  • Continue Watching Tray: Dynamically displays in-progress shows with the exact next unwatched episode card for single-tap playback resumption.
  • Social Friends Progress: View which friends are currently watching the show and where they are (e.g. "@alex is on S02E08"), avoiding spoilers while coordinating watch parties.

4.4 Ranked Stacks (The Cinephile Report Card)

Numbered, curated collections (e.g., "Top 10 Mind-Benders of All Time", "Favorite Nolan Films"). Includes tactile drag-and-drop reordering with single-transaction backend synchronization. Stacks can be exported as 9:16 social cards and scanned via QR codes.

4.5 Attributed Watchlists

When a user adds a film recommended by a friend or scanned from a QR code, the recommender's identity is permanently attached. When the film is finally logged, the original recommender receives recognition and an impact score boost.

4.6 Peer Suggestions & Gratitude Loop

Send movies or shows directly to friends with a heartfelt note. Grouped inbox clusters recommendations by movie. When watched, the app prompts: "Send a note of thanks to @sarah?", closing the social gratitude loop.

4.7 SmartSuggest AI Engine

Proprietary recommendation algorithm balancing social influence, consensus acclaim, personal taste profile, and binge fatigue avoidance: 

Score (S) = (w_friend * F) + (w_critic * C) + (w_taste * T) + B_diversity - P_fatigue

3 Modes: Hive Mind (Friend focus), Certified Gold (Critic consensus), and Palate Cleanser (Diversity focus).

4.8 MindLens 2.0 Behavioral Telemetry

Measures cinematic taste breadth using Shannon Information Entropy: 

H(G) = - Σ [ p(g_i) * log2(p(g_i)) ]

Classifies viewers across 6 cognitive archetypes (Cinema Polymath, Deep Diver, Adrenaline Seeker, Comfort Voyager, Cerebral Analyst, Contrarian Explorer) and maps mood onto Russell's Circumplex Affect Plane.

4.9 Social Swarm Feed

Chronological community activity feed with cursor pagination, nested comments, likes, and smart "Who to Follow" recommendations based on shared genres and mutual friends.

4.10 Social Taste Compare

Compare your cinematic footprint with any other user on the platform: Venn overlap of mutually watched films, rating correlation delta, and shared favorites.

4.11 Unified Watch Preferences Survey

A single, non-repeating onboarding survey that gathers preferred genres, cinematic eras, mood inclinations, and active streaming subscriptions. Powering immediate cold-start personalization.

4.12 Cinephile XP, Streaks & Honey Badges

Constructive gamification: +20 XP for logging, +30 XP for thoughtful reviews (100+ chars), +50 XP for stacks, and +75 XP when friends watch your suggestions. Unlocks 8 collectible Honey Badges.

4.13 QR Codes & Universal Deep Links

Custom scheme watchhive:// and Universal Links (https://watchershive.com/) with native fallback. Scanning a QR code opens the exact profile, movie, or stack natively in the app, or surfaces an "Open in App" smart web banner.

⚠️ The Golden Contrast Rule (Mandatory Across All Platforms)
All text and icons rendered on Honey Gold (#FFB700), Cream (#FFF9F0), or White (#FFFFFF) surfaces MUST be Charcoal Black (#2D2926). NEVER render white text on gold or cream surfaces.

Loader Hierarchy

  • Primary: Skeleton Loaders (WHSkeletonBox): Mandatory everywhere while fetching data (cards, carousels, lists, feed). Preserves layout stability and prevents content shifting.
  • Secondary: Animated Bee Loader (BeeLoader): Used ONLY for cold app boot, splash screen, or modal submit operations where skeletons cannot fit.

Rating Scale

Strict 10-point scale (0.5 to 10.0), visualized as 5 dual-halved honey stars. Strictly identical across picker, cards, and profile stats.

🔐 6. Authentication & Session Security

WatchersHive is 100% Passwordless. Traditional passwords have been phased out completely to eliminate phishing, credential stuffing, and forgotten password frustration.

Authentication Methods

Method Flow Security Characteristics
6-Digit Email OTP User enters email $\rightarrow$ 6-digit code sent $\rightarrow$ User submits code. Cryptographically random OTP, 10-minute expiry, hashed in PostgreSQL, automatic verification.
Google OAuth 2.0 Google One Tap / Native Google Sign-In SDK. ID token verified on backend via Google tokeninfo and official library.
Apple ID Native Sign in with Apple SDK. Identity token validated via Apple public keys, captures name on initial authorization.

Stateless JWT Lifecycle

Access tokens (7 days) are verified statelessly without database lookups on routine requests, preventing API bottlenecks and eliminating random logouts. Refresh tokens (90 days) are rotated at /api/v1/auth/refresh where global session revocation (tokenVersion) is cleanly enforced.

⚙️ 7. Technical Architecture & Performance

TMDb SWR Caching & Single-Flight Deduplication

All TMDb endpoints (movies, TV, recommendations, trending, search) are protected by a high-performance caching layer:

  • Public Access: Media routes use optionalAuthMiddleware, enabling instant guest browsing and eliminating 401 barriers.
  • Single-Flight Deduplication: Identical concurrent requests are batched into a single outbound TMDb call, eliminating duplicate external requests.
  • Edge CDN Headers: Downstream proxies and CDNs cache responses with stale-while-revalidate headers for sub-30ms delivery.
  • Multi-Tier In-Memory Caching: User profiles (30s), stats summaries (60s), and friend recommendations (60s) are cached with event-driven invalidation.

⚖️ 8. Legal, Privacy & Compliance

ℹ️ Non-Commercial & Fan-Made Operation Disclosure
WatchersHive is an independent, non-commercial, fan-made social movie and TV tracking community application built strictly for educational, personal cataloging, critique, and commentary purposes. WatchersHive generates zero revenue, displays zero commercial advertisements, sells no user data or merchandise, and charges no fees or subscriptions.

Terms of Service Summary

By using WatchersHive, users agree to the Terms of Service:

  • Eligibility: Users must be at least 13 years of age (16 years in the EEA/UK).
  • Content Ownership: Users retain ownership of reviews, ratings, and stacks they author, granting WatchersHive a non-exclusive license to display this content on the Hive feed.
  • Fair Use & Commentary: All discussions, ratings, and reviews represent fair-use personal opinions under copyright laws.

Privacy Policy & Telemetry

WatchersHive is committed to data minimization:

  • Data Collected: Email address (for authentication), username, display name, watch logs, ratings, reviews, and custom stacks.
  • Telemetry: MindLens analytics (Shannon Entropy, mood distribution) are computed solely to reflect taste back to the user and are never commercialized or shared with third parties.
  • Cookies & Storage: Minimal essential cookies/storage (access token, refresh token) required for session persistence. No tracking cookies or advertising pixels.

GDPR & CCPA Rights

Under the European GDPR and California CCPA:

  • Right to Access & Portability: Users can export their full watch history and reviews as JSON/CSV.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Users can permanently delete their account and all associated entries, ratings, and comments at any time via Profile Settings.

🚀 10. DevOps & Release Management

When implementing cross-platform features, follow the 3-Step Staging Pipeline:

1. Backend First

Update schema.ts, create service/controller, run npm test, merge staging PR to trigger Railway deployment.

2. Web Frontend Second

Update TypeScript types, add service call, build skeleton UI, test build with npm run build, merge PR to trigger Vercel deploy.

3. Mobile Third

Update Dart models, add API endpoint constant, update Riverpod notifier, test with flutter analyze and build APK/iOS.
